A Home for Your Brand

Display your products in the front of our Healing Hub at 19562 Grand River Ave in Detroit — a dedicated, curated retail space designed to showcase your work beautifully alongside other Black women-owned businesses.

We Handle the Sales

TSC staff manage all customer transactions on your behalf — so you don't have to be on-site. We process each sale, track inventory in real time, and send you a detailed monthly statement with every payout.

A 3-Month Rotation

Each placement term is 3 months. When the term concludes, all vendor slots rotate back to the applicant pool — keeping the Retail Space fresh, giving more businesses a chance, and bringing repeat visitors back to see what's new.

The Process

How It Works

From application to payout, here's what to expect at every stage of your time with us.

01

Apply

Complete our online vendor application. Share photos of your products, your website, your customer reach, and your prior vendor experience. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.

02

Get Selected

TSC reviews each application for wellness alignment, product quality, and category fit. You'll hear back within 10 business days. If your category is already filled for the current term, you'll be added to the waitlist.

03

Sign & Drop Off

Once approved, you'll sign your Vendor Agreement, pay your $150 security deposit, and bring your products to the Hub. We'll complete a signed Product Intake Form together at drop-off.

04

Sell & Get Paid

Your products go on display and TSC manages all sales. On the 15th of each month, you receive your payout — gross sales minus the 25% commission — plus a full sales statement.

Program Terms

What You Need to Know

Simple, transparent terms designed to make this work for everyone — especially you.

Admin Fee
$150
A one-time, non-refundable fee due at signing. Covers onboarding, intake, and POS setup for your full term. No monthly rent — nothing else owed beyond commission.
Sales Commission
25%
TSC retains 25% of the gross sale price of each item sold. The remaining 75% of each sale goes directly to you. No rent deducted — commission is your only ongoing cost. TSC collects and remits all sales tax.
Placement Term
3 months
All placements are 3-month terms. Terms do not renew automatically. All vendor slots rotate back to the applicant pool at term end.
No Monthly Rent
$0/mo
After your admin fee, there are no recurring monthly charges. Your only ongoing obligation is the 25% commission on each item sold — paid from your proceeds automatically.
Vendor Payout
The 15th
Monthly payout on the 15th for the prior month's sales — gross sales minus 25% commission. You receive a detailed statement with every payment.
Product Retrieval
7 days
Unsold products must be picked up within 7 days of your term end date. We'll notify you 14 days in advance so you're prepared.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Still deciding? Here are answers to the questions we hear most often.

Do I have to be at the Hub to sell my products?
No. TSC staff manage all customer-facing sales on your behalf. You don't need to be present. You do need to visit periodically to restock your display area — we'll reach out if inventory looks low, but restocking on your own schedule is your responsibility.
What if sales are slow one month?
There is no monthly rent — your only financial obligation beyond the one-time $150 admin fee is the 25% commission on each item sold. If your products don't move, TSC earns less commission, but you won't be invoiced for anything additional. This is why we carefully curate vendors and encourage applicants to bring a product line with demonstrated demand.
Can I restock during the term?
Yes, absolutely. You can drop off restocked inventory anytime during Hub operating hours. You'll need to check in with staff and complete an updated Product Intake Form for any new items. Keeping your space well-stocked is your responsibility — and a key part of success in this program.
Can I sell items that require refrigeration?
No. TSC only accepts non-perishable products. This includes food items, fresh flowers, and any product with an expiration date occurring within the placement term. Sealed skincare with a manufacturer expiration at least 6 months out is acceptable.
What happens to my unsold products at the end?
You'll be notified 14 days before your term ends. Unsold products must be picked up within 7 days of the term end date. Items not retrieved by the deadline may be donated or discarded at TSC's discretion. We want your products in your hands — please plan ahead.
Can I renew my vendor spot for another term?
Not automatically. All vendor slots rotate back to the applicant pool at the end of each 3-month term. If you'd like to participate in the next term, you're welcome to submit a new application. This rotation keeps the Retail Space fresh and gives more Black women-owned businesses the opportunity to participate.
Is TSC responsible if my products are damaged or stolen?
TSC is not liable for loss, theft, or damage to vendor inventory. The Hub takes reasonable precautions, but vendors assume this risk when delivering products. We strongly encourage all vendors to carry general liability and product liability insurance before the term begins.
Who sets the prices for my products?
You do. Vendors set all retail prices. You'll provide TSC with a current product price list at intake, and we'll tag everything accordingly. If you need to change a price during the term, notify us at least 5 business days in advance so we can update the system accurately.
